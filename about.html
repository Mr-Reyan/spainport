WHO WE ARE

About Ship Chandler In Spain

A dedicated ship chandlery partner focused on the everyday needs of vessels and their crews.

Our Company

Your trusted partner at the Port of Valencia.

Ship Chandler in Spain Co., Ltd supports vessel teams with a focused, coordinated approach to marine procurement and ship provisioning.

Ship Chandler in Spain delivers end-to-end maritime procurement and professional vessel provisioning for ships calling at the Port of Valencia and surrounding anchorages. We work closely alongside fleet managers, shipowners, technical superintendents, purchasing executives, ship masters, and local shipping agencies to ensure seamless, punctual onboard delivery throughout the vessel's port turnaround.

Our commitment is to function as a unified, reliable agency desk for every vessel supply need in Spanish waters. Whether delivering prime galley provisions, bonded goods, deck consumables, or technical engine-room spares, our dispatch desk synchronizes each order precisely with the ship's confirmed ETA, dock assignment, port timetable, and individual requisition list.

Ship Chandler in Spain operates strictly as dedicated marine suppliers rather than general freight forwarding agents. Our core specialization centers exclusively on ship chandlery: fresh food provisions, galley stores, deck and engine supplies, bonded stores, crew beverages, and cabin consumables.
